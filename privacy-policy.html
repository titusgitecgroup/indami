Privacy Policy

INDAMI™ is a product of IMC Technologies Inc. This notice explains what information we collect when you visit www.indami.io, how we use it, and your choices.

What We Collect

When you submit a form on this site, such as a waitlist signup or an Enterprise Pilot Programme application, we collect the information you provide—including your name, business email address, phone number, company name, and any other details included in the form.

When you book an Enterprise conversation via our Google Calendar booking page, we collect your name, business email address, phone number, company name, and role/title.

When you visit this site, we automatically collect certain technical information, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. We use Google Analytics to collect this information in aggregated, anonymized form to help us understand how visitors use the site.

How We Use It

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you about your waitlist position or Enterprise Pilot Programme application
  • Send you updates about INDAMI™, including product availability, launch timing, and early access opportunities
  • Improve the performance and content of this website

We do not sell your information to anyone.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with the service providers that help us operate this site and communicate with you—including our analytics provider and email platform. These providers are only permitted to use your information to perform services on our behalf.

Your Choices

Email communications: Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time. You may still receive a direct reply if you submitted an application or inquiry.

Cookies: You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use this site.

A Note on Platform Privacy

This notice covers the INDAMI™ website and its waitlist and early access forms only. When the INDAMI™ platform launches and you create an account, a full Privacy Policy will be presented at the time of account creation. That policy will cover how we handle data within the platform, including uploaded files, session data, and user accounts.
